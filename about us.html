Who We Are

El Ashry Furniture was established to become one of the leading companies specialized in the production of both modern and classic Egyptian furniture. We aim to provide products that combine high quality, elegant design, and affordable prices.

Our Vision

To become a leading brand in the furniture industry in Egypt and beyond, while preserving the Egyptian heritage of furniture craftsmanship and continuously evolving with the latest global trends.

Our Services

  • Design and production of modern and classic bedroom sets
  • High-quality and ergonomic office furniture
  • Custom-made furniture tailored to client needs
  • Shipping and delivery to all governorates

Why Choose Us?

Because we believe that furniture is more than just wooden pieces — it’s part of your comfort and the beauty of your home. We use the finest materials, advanced manufacturing techniques, and a team of highly skilled craftsmen and designers.
